ACCOUNTING AND INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Values are taken directly from the published ALL STATE TAXES
   row in Washington DOR Tax Statistics, Table 2.
2. DOR net tax collections are used as published. This extraction
   does not independently reconstruct individual tax categories.
3. Washington State fiscal years end June 30; federal fiscal years
   end September 30. Year labels do not imply identical periods.
4. This series is contextual. It is not an additional federal
   inflow or outflow and must not alter federal balance arithmetic.
5. Spreadsheet numeric values are read through openpyxl as Python
   numbers, converted using Decimal(str(value)), multiplied by
   1,000, and rounded to cents using ROUND_HALF_UP.
6. The cents are normalized spreadsheet precision, not an
   independent certification of cash receipts to the cent.
7. The DOR published total may include items such as penalties
   and interest. Preserve the published measure definition.
8. Original source workbooks are retained without modification.
