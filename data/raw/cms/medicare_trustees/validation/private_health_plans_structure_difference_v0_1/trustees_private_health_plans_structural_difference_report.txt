Actuarial Methodology 148 b. Historical Table IV.C1 shows historical and p rojected private health plan enrollment by type of plan. Between 2009 and 2018, private plan enrollment grew by 10.2 million or 92 percent, compared to growth in the overall Medicare population of 29 percent for the same period. PFFS enrollment dropped 94 percent between 2009 and 2018 primarily due to plan reaction to new statutory provider network requirements beginning in 2011 . Most of the enrollees in terminating PFFS plans transferred to LCCP or RPPO plans.
