QUESTION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What annual period does the CMS Original Medicare Geographic Variation PUF YEAR variable represent?

DOCUMENTARY EVIDENCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dictionary: YEAR is defined as Year.
Methods Paper: the FFS GV PUF explicitly covers calendar years.
Methods Paper: study-population descriptions independently use calendar-year terminology.

VINTAGE BOUNDARY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The preserved May 2024 Methods Paper directly documents the PUF through calendar year 2022.
The current WCFFBP source extends through 2024. This certification applies the documented dataset annual-period convention to those later observations; it does not claim that the preserved Methods Paper individually documents CY2023 or CY2024.

VALIDATION CHECKS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PASS  DICTIONARY_YEAR_FIELD: CMS data dictionary identifies YEAR as the source year field [Expected extracted dictionary evidence equivalent to Term Name=Year; Variable Name=YEAR; Definition=Year]
PASS  METHODS_CALENDAR_YEAR_STATEMENT: CMS Methods Paper explicitly states that the FFS GV PUF covers calendar years [Expected phrase containing "covers calendar years"]
PASS  METHODS_CALENDAR_YEAR_POPULATION: CMS Methods Paper applies calendar-year terminology to the FFS GV PUF study population [Expected phrase containing "during the calendar year"]
PASS  TIMING_BASIS_RESOLUTION: Source annual-period convention resolves to CALENDAR_YEAR [resolved_timing_basis=CALENDAR_YEAR]
PASS  METHODS_VINTAGE_BOUNDARY: Preserved Methods Paper is recognized as directly documenting the PUF through 2022 [Later 2023-2024 observations inherit the documented PUF annual-period convention; they are not individually documented by the preserved May 2024 Methods Paper]

TIMING INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CMS Original Medicare Geographic Variation YEAR is certified as CALENDAR_YEAR.

Accordingly, the WCFFBP Original Medicare annual observations may be aligned by calendar year with the certified Part C and Part D calendar-month reconstructions.

No conversion from calendar year to federal fiscal year is performed or authorized by this certification.
