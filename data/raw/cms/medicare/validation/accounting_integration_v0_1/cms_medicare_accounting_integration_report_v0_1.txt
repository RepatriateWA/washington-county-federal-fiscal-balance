INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The three principal Washington Medicare streams are assembled without changing their certified source values.
The resulting core Medicare sum is an accounting-integration candidate, not yet a certified final ledger value.
Trustees controls are retained separately because accounting scope, component, basis, and timing comparability must be established before reconciliation.
CY2024 must not be interpreted as a complete three-stream annual total because Part C and Part D contain Jan-Sep only.
Part D remains partial-dollar coverage in every year represented by the frozen reconstruction.
