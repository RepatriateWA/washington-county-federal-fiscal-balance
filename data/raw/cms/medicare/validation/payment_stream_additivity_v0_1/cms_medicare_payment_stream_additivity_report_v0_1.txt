CMS MEDICARE PAYMENT-STREAM ADDITIVITY CERTIFICATION v0.1
==============================================================================

ACCOUNTING INVARIANT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
No federal expenditure source is additive by default.
A plus sign is permitted only after affirmative evidence that the candidate represents an economically distinct federal payment stream.

PRINCIPAL STREAMS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ORIGINAL_MEDICARE_FFS
  Component: Original Medicare / Fee-for-Service
  Benefit scope: Medicare Part A and Part B
  Payment scope: Actual Original Medicare / FFS payments represented by TOT_MDCR_PYMT_AMT in the CMS Geographic Variation PUF
  Source: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation by National, State & County
  Prior certification: Original Medicare / FFS source mechanics v0.2 PASS

MEDICARE_PART_C
  Component: Medicare Advantage Part C
  Benefit scope: Medicare Part A/B benefits delivered through Medicare Advantage plans
  Payment scope: Risk-adjusted A/B plan payment plus Part C rebate
  Source: CMS Plan Payment PUF + monthly CPSC enrollment
  Prior certification: Washington Medicare Advantage Part C reconstruction v0.2 PASS

MEDICARE_PART_D
  Component: Medicare Part D
  Benefit scope: Medicare outpatient prescription-drug benefit
  Payment scope: Risk-adjusted Direct Subsidy plus Reinsurance plus Low-Income Cost Sharing subsidy
  Source: CMS Plan Payment PUF + monthly CPSC enrollment
  Prior certification: Washington Medicare Part D reconstruction v0.3 PASS

PAIRWISE OVERLAP MATRIX
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ORIGINAL_MEDICARE_FFS <-> MEDICARE_PART_C
  Beneficiary overlap possible: NO_FOR_SAME_COVERAGE_MONTH
  Payment-stream overlap: NO
  Classification: ADDITIVE
  Basis: Original Medicare/FFS represents Part A/B claims for the traditional FFS population. Part C represents Medicare Advantage plan payments for Part A/B benefits delivered through MA plans. These are alternative Part A/B delivery systems rather than two representations of the same payment.
  Boundary: Do not infer additivity merely from different datasets. Additivity rests on mutually distinct Part A/B payment delivery streams.

ORIGINAL_MEDICARE_FFS <-> MEDICARE_PART_D
  Beneficiary overlap possible: YES
  Payment-stream overlap: NO
  Classification: ADDITIVE
  Basis: An Original Medicare beneficiary may also enroll in Part D, so beneficiary populations can overlap. The payment streams do not: the FFS Geographic Variation expenditure measure is the Original Medicare Part A/B stream, whereas Part D is the prescription-drug subsidy stream reconstructed from Direct Subsidy, Reinsurance, and LICS.
  Boundary: Beneficiary overlap is not payment-dollar overlap.

MEDICARE_PART_C <-> MEDICARE_PART_D
  Beneficiary overlap possible: YES
  Payment-stream overlap: NO
  Classification: ADDITIVE
  Basis: MA-PD beneficiaries may generate both Part C and Part D payments. Certified Part C reconstruction consists of risk-adjusted A/B payment plus rebate. Certified Part D reconstruction consists of risk-adjusted Direct Subsidy, Reinsurance, and LICS. The components are economically distinct federal payment streams.
  Boundary: Shared contract, plan, or beneficiary identity does not make Part C and Part D payments the same dollars.

KNOWN NON-ADDITIVE REPRESENTATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART_D_RECONCILIATION_WORKBOOK -> MEDICARE_PART_D
  Classification: CONTROL_ONLY
  Reason: Alternative reconciliation representation of Part D; not a second additive expenditure stream.

USASPENDING_ASSISTANCE_93.774 -> MEDICARE_SMI
  Classification: OVERLAPPING_EXCLUDE_DUPLICATE
  Reason: Aggregate Medicare SMI reporting retained for provenance and reconciliation; not additive to CMS-native Medicare.

CONCLUSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For the certified CMS-native payment streams evaluated here:

  Medicare = Original Medicare/FFS + Part C + Part D

The plus signs refer to economically distinct federal payment streams, not mutually exclusive beneficiary populations.

This certification does NOT assert that these three streams constitute the complete Medicare federal inflow universe.
RDS and any other candidate Medicare payment streams require their own accounting-source/additivity gates before inclusion.
