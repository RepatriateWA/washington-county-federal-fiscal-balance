ACCOUNTING INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Original Medicare/FFS, Part C, and Part D are certified as additive payment streams within the defined core Medicare accounting boundary.
Beneficiary overlap does not imply payment-dollar overlap.
The core boundary is not asserted to contain every Medicare-related federal expenditure.
MSSP EarnSaveLoss is excluded as duplicate under the separately certified overlap policy.
RDS and Washington MFFS are additive in principle but remain withheld because their Washington/county dollar incidence or annual timing is unresolved.
Trustees evidence is diagnostic accounting evidence and is not used to scale or force-balance Washington reconstructed dollars.
CY2015-CY2023 have complete calendar-year time windows, but Part D retains PARTIAL_DOLLAR_COVERAGE.
CY2024 is not a complete annual core Medicare dollar total because Part C and Part D contain Jan-Sep only.
