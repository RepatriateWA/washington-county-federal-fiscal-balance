CMS MDCR ENROLL D 3 WASHINGTON RESIDENCE CERTIFICATION v0.3
==============================================================================

Scope: CY2015-CY2023

CERTIFIED SEMANTIC BOUNDARY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MDCR ENROLL D 3 publishes Retiree Drug Subsidy (No Part D Plan) enrollment by Area of Residence.
Washington is directly observed as a published residence geography.
The source therefore supports a Washington-resident RDS enrollment series for CY2015-CY2023.

NON-CERTIFIED INFERENCES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This source does not establish Washington RDS payment dollars.
RDS beneficiary counts are not treated as payment-dollar allocation weights.
This source does not establish county-level Washington RDS enrollment or payments.
