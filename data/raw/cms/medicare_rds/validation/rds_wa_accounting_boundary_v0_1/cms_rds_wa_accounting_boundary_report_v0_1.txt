ACCOUNTING BOUNDARY
====================================================================================================

FEDERAL_EXPENDITURE_EXISTENCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Status: OBSERVED
Evidence: CMS Plan Payment Data publishes Retiree Drug Subsidy plan payment data by plan year.
Ledger implication: RDS is a known federal expenditure stream; it must not be treated as nonexistent or zero.

PAYMENT_DOLLAR_GRAIN
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Status: SPONSOR
Evidence: The public CMS RDS Plan Payment PUF reports payment information by plan sponsor/business rather than by beneficiary residence.
Ledger implication: Sponsor identity cannot be used as beneficiary geography.

ECONOMIC_ADDITIVITY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Status: ADDITIVE_IN_PRINCIPLE
Evidence: Prior WCFFBP RDS semantic/additivity certification found RDS payment dollars economically distinct from Original Medicare/FFS, Part C, and Part D payment streams.
Ledger implication: RDS is not excluded as a duplicate of FFS, Part C, or Part D.

WASHINGTON_BENEFICIARY_INCIDENCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Status: DIRECTLY_OBSERVED
Evidence: CMS MDCR ENROLL D 3 publishes Retiree Drug Subsidy (No Part D Plan) enrollment by Area of Residence. WCFFBP v0.3 certified Washington observations for CY2015-CY2023.
Ledger implication: Washington RDS beneficiary incidence is established, but beneficiary counts are not payment dollars.

WASHINGTON_DOLLAR_INCIDENCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Status: UNRESOLVED
Evidence: No validated public CMS source has been identified that links actual RDS subsidy dollars to beneficiary residence in Washington.
Ledger implication: Actual RDS dollars cannot currently be assigned to Washington in the principal ledger.

WASHINGTON_COUNTY_DOLLAR_INCIDENCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Status: UNRESOLVED
Evidence: No validated public CMS source has been identified that reports or links actual RDS subsidy dollars to Washington county beneficiary residence.
Ledger implication: No county RDS dollar allocation is permitted.

BENEFICIARY_COUNT_ALLOCATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Status: REJECTED_FOR_PRINCIPAL_LEDGER
Evidence: CMS RDS subsidy payments depend on allowable retiree prescription-drug costs and are not a fixed payment per RDS beneficiary.
Ledger implication: Washington's share of RDS beneficiaries must not be used as though it were observed Washington payment incidence.

SPONSOR_NAME_GEOGRAPHY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Status: REJECTED
Evidence: RDS PUF business/sponsor identity describes the payment recipient/sponsor and does not establish beneficiary residence.
Ledger implication: Sponsor names, including geographically named sponsors, must not be converted into Washington expenditure incidence.

PRINCIPAL_LEDGER_TREATMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Status: WITHHOLD_UNRESOLVED
Evidence: Economic additivity has been established, but Washington payment-dollar geographic incidence has not.
Ledger implication: Withhold RDS from the principal Washington ledger pending a validated geographic-dollar source.

ZERO_INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Status: PROHIBITED
Evidence: Withholding results from unresolved geographic attribution, not evidence of zero Washington RDS expenditure.
Ledger implication: Do not encode withheld RDS as $0.

OVERALL RDS WASHINGTON ACCOUNTING BOUNDARY: PASS
====================================================================================================

Certified boundary:
  Federal RDS expenditure: OBSERVED
  Public payment-dollar grain: SPONSOR
  Economic status: ADDITIVE_IN_PRINCIPLE
  Washington beneficiary incidence: DIRECTLY_OBSERVED
  Washington dollar incidence: UNRESOLVED
  Washington county dollar incidence: UNRESOLVED
  Principal ledger treatment: WITHHOLD_UNRESOLVED

Methodological interpretation:
  RDS is a known and economically distinct federal Medicare expenditure stream.
  Public CMS data establish actual RDS subsidy payments and independently establish RDS beneficiary residence, but do not provide a validated public linkage between subsidy dollars and beneficiary residence.
  WCFFBP therefore does not allocate RDS payments to Washington using beneficiary counts or sponsor identity.
  RDS is withheld from the principal Washington ledger pending a validated geographic-dollar source.
  Withholding does not imply zero Washington RDS expenditure.

Rejected principal-ledger estimator:

  RDS_WA = RDS_US * (RDS_Beneficiaries_WA / RDS_Beneficiaries_US)

Reason: RDS subsidy dollars depend on allowable retiree prescription-drug costs rather than a fixed payment per beneficiary. Such a calculation would be a model, not an observed Washington expenditure.

Accounting invariant:

  Economic additivity does not imply geographic allocatability.
  No federal expenditure is geographically attributable by default.
  Geographic attribution must be demonstrated independently of expenditure existence and economic additivity.
