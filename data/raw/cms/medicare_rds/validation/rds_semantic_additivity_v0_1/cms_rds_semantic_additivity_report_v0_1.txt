CMS RETIREE DRUG SUBSIDY SEMANTIC / ADDITIVITY CERTIFICATION v0.1
==============================================================================

SEMANTIC CONCLUSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RDS is a federal Medicare prescription-drug subsidy payment to qualified employer/union retiree plan sponsors.
A qualifying covered retiree is eligible for Medicare Part D but is not enrolled in a Medicare Part D plan.

ADDITIVITY CONCLUSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RDS is economically distinct from the WCFFBP-certified Original Medicare/FFS, Part C, and Part D payment streams.

Medicare evaluated streams, if geographic incidence is resolved:
  Original Medicare/FFS + Part C + Part D + RDS

GEOGRAPHIC BOUNDARY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The profiled public RDS Plan Payment table contains sponsor/business identity, payment amount, and retiree count.
It does not contain an explicit state, county, ZIP, or beneficiary-residence field usable for WCFFBP allocation.
A geographic word appearing inside Business Name does not constitute geographic evidence.
