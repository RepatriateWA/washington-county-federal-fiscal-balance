\# Washington County Federal Fiscal Balance Database (WCFFBD)



\## Project Overview



The Washington County Federal Fiscal Balance Database (WCFFBD) is an open research project that estimates the annual federal fiscal balance for each county in Washington State.



The project integrates data from multiple federal sources to estimate:



\- Federal taxes collected

\- Federal expenditures received

\- Net federal fiscal balance

\- Per-capita fiscal balance

\- Fiscal balance as a percentage of county personal income



\## Objectives



\- Build a transparent, reproducible research database.

\- Document every data source and transformation.

\- Produce county-level fiscal balance estimates for Washington State.

\- Enable future expansion to all U.S. counties.



\## Planned Data Sources



\- U.S. Census Bureau

\- Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA)

\- Internal Revenue Service (IRS)

\- USAspending.gov

\- Social Security Administration

\- Department of Veterans Affairs

\- Bureau of Labor Statistics

\- Federal Procurement Data
