# Source Inventory

## Rockefeller

| Document | Years | Downloaded | Notes |
|-----------|-------|------------|------|
| 2019 Balance of Payments Report | FFY 2017 / revised prior estimates | No | Early Rockefeller BOP methodology; two-step allocation from federal budget categories to states. |
| 2020 Balance of Payments Report | FFY 2018 / revised prior estimates | No | Pre-COVID methodology baseline. |
| 2021 Balance of Payments Report | FFY 2019 / revised prior estimates | No | Last mainly pre-COVID report. |
| 2023 Balance of Payments Report | FFY 2020–2021 COVID period | No | Explains COVID-era distortion and allocable federal budget approach. |
| 2024 Balance of Payments Report | FFY 2021–2022 | No | Includes “with COVID” / “without COVID” comparison framework. |
| 2025 Balance of Payments Report | FFY 2015–2023 | No | Best current methodology source; includes detailed receipts, expenditures, and COVID spending tables. |

Rockefeller does not simply use IRS Table 5 gross collections as state receipts. Its method breaks federal budget receipts and expenditures into categories, then allocates each category to states using actual state data when available or proxy allocators when not available. State totals are constrained to federal budget totals.
