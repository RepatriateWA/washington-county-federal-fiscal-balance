Annual receipts and inflow reconciliation

The currently available annual reporting file provides aggregate inflows and the Medicare Part D increment. It does not provide a source-family decomposition for Social Security, Medicare A/B/C, Medicaid, CHIP, VA, federal salaries, or assistance. Those categories are not invented or inferred here; they can be added after retrieving the source-family bridge.

COVID-related federal inflows

Not yet quantified in this chart. COVID-related spending is embedded in some reported inflow families, but the annual reporting CSV does not identify a comprehensive, mutually exclusive COVID subtotal. An additive COVID overlay would risk double-counting. We can add a separate COVID attribution chart once source-family tags and overlap decisions are reconciled.

The pandemic affected both federal transfers and tax collections. Annual changes in the balance cannot be attributed entirely to COVID without a counterfactual and verified program classification.

Accounting status and limitations

IRS net collections are qualified provisional; Medicare Part D addition is qualified and has partial dollar coverage; FY2024 Medicare Part C has partial-year coverage. Source time bases are mixed. Economic incidence and completeness remain unresolved. This visualization does not alter the canonical ledger or authorize any source-family amount.
