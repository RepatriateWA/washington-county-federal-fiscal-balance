How to interpret these figures

The stacked bars reconcile exactly to the existing ETL-243E1 Provisional Reference (before rounding for display). They combine annualized Social Security December snapshots, calendar-year Medicare observations, federal fiscal-year program measures, and policy-classified assistance obligations. Medicare Part C has partial January–September coverage for 2024. Medicare Part D and additional assistance are qualified additions. Net IRS collections are a provisional gross-minus-refunds treatment.

Procurement obligations, agency-level DoD/DOE/Hanford expenditures, civilian and military retirement, and SSI are not added to these bars unless established in the reference calculation. COVID/IIJA tags are subsets of assistance, not extra additive categories. The bars are a reconciliation of identified federal activity, not a complete measurement of federal cash entering Washington.

Sources: ETL-238A source-family bridge; ETL-238B8 expanded assistance; ETL-242A5 Medicare Part D; ETL-243E1 provisional reference; ETL-244A5 component extraction. No database or canonical-ledger changes.
