PROJECT OBJECTIVE
-----------------
Construct a transparent, reproducible, evidence-linked Washington State
federal fiscal balance for FY2018–FY2024, with later allocation across
Washington's 39 counties. Compare federal dollars withdrawn (net federal
taxes collected) with federal dollars expended or otherwise entering the
state. Negative balance = net dollars withdrawn from Washington.
Guiding principle: "Measure first. Explain later."
The work is ongoing and provisional snapshots are useful for collaborators,
provided coverage, measure basis, timing, and uncertainty are disclosed.
