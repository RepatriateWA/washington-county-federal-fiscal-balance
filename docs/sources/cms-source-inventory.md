# CMS Source Inventory

## Medicare

### Medicare Geographic Variation — National, State & County

**Agency:** Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services  
**Program:** Medicare  
**Preferred geography:** County  
**Primary use:** Original Medicare beneficiary counts and spending measures  
**Time basis:** Calendar/data year, as published  
**Coverage limitation:** Original Medicare population; Medicare Advantage is not fully represented  
**Warehouse role:** County-level Medicare expenditure source

Planned years:

- 2015
- 2016
- 2017
- 2018
- 2019
- 2020
- 2021
- 2022
- 2023
- 2024, if available in downloadable form

## Medicaid

### MBES/CBES Expenditure Reports

**Agency:** Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services  
**Program:** Medicaid  
**Preferred geography:** State  
**Primary use:** Actual Medicaid expenditures and federal financial participation  
**Time basis:** Federal fiscal year  
**Warehouse role:** Authoritative Washington Medicaid federal expenditure control totals  
**County allocation:** To be developed separately using official enrollment or claims-based geography

Planned years:

- FY2015 through FY2024

## CHIP

### CMS-21 Annual CHIP Expenditure Reports

**Agency:** Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services  
**Program:** CHIP  
**Preferred geography:** State  
**Primary use:** Federal and total CHIP expenditures  
**Time basis:** Federal fiscal year  
**Warehouse role:** Authoritative Washington CHIP federal expenditure totals

Planned years:

- FY2015 through FY2024

## Double-Counting Rule

CMS program totals will be the preferred source for Medicare, Medicaid, and CHIP.

Overlapping USAspending records will not be added to CMS totals unless they are demonstrated to represent distinct expenditures.
