# IRS Gross Collections ? Qualified Provisional Authorization

## Scope

Family: IRS_GROSS_COLLECTIONS

Fiscal years: 2018?2024

Direction: OUTFLOW

Status: QUALIFIED_PROVISIONAL_OUTFLOW

## Authorized measure

Washington-attributed IRS gross collections, as represented in
reporting.v_washington_revenue_summary.

This is an administrative collections control.

It is not a final estimate of federal tax economic incidence
for Washington residents.

## Verification

ETL-225K: Seven-year statewide revenue observations reproduced.

ETL-227C: Source SQL and annual registry observations reviewed.

ETL-227C2: All seven reporting-view comparisons returned zero
differences.

## Remaining qualification

Geographic collection attribution and economic tax incidence
are distinct concepts.

The administrative collections measure may be used in a
provisional fiscal statement only when labeled accordingly.

It must not be described as the final tax burden borne by
Washington residents.

## Accounting treatment

Seven annual amounts authorized as qualified provisional
administrative outflow controls.

No other source families authorized by this decision.

## Regression requirement

All other family-year observations and accounting statuses
must remain unchanged.
