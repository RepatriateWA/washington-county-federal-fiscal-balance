# WCFFBP Draft 1 Statewide Evidence

This directory preserves the principal analytical evidence generated during
development of the Washington County Federal Fiscal Balance Project (WCFFBP)
Draft 1 statewide accounting exercise.

## Status

Draft 1 is a preliminary analytical exercise, not a final certified Washington
federal fiscal balance.

The artifacts preserved here are read-only analytical outputs. They do not
authorize persistent database changes, permanent accounting classifications,
unsupported timing conversions, missing-value imputations, or county-level
economic-incidence conclusions.

## Files

### draft_1a_assistance_bridge.txt

Reconstructs the certified USAspending assistance universe and applies existing
accounting decisions together with explicitly provisional Draft-1 treatment of
previously unresolved COVID, IIJA, and other extraordinary transactions.

The output verifies that each certified transaction appears exactly once in the
accounting bridge. Provisional classifications remain provisional and do not
constitute permanent accounting policy.

### draft_1b_component_extraction.txt

Extracts statewide fiscal-balance components used in the Draft-1 analytical
exercise.

The execution is read-only and makes no new accounting decisions or
county-incidence inferences.

### draft_1c_procurement_profile.txt

Profiles the canonical USAspending procurement universe and validates important
transaction, award-linkage, uniqueness, and required-field controls.

The execution is read-only. It does not itself make a procurement accounting
decision or county-incidence inference.

### statewide_readiness_v0_2.txt

Preserves the Washington Statewide Federal Fiscal Balance Readiness Audit v0.2.

This audit distinguishes streams suitable for preliminary inclusion from
unresolved, duplicate, or reference-only streams and records the remaining
analytical priorities before final statewide certification.

The preliminary accounting view documented by the audit is informational and
must not be interpreted as a final certified Washington federal fiscal balance.

### statewide_reporting_definitions.txt

Preserves the reporting-view definitions used during the Draft-1 analytical
exercise.

This artifact provides technical provenance for the reporting logic and should
be interpreted together with the dated analytical outputs above rather than as
a current or independently certified fiscal-balance result.

## Methodological Principle

WCFFBP separates:

1. source measurement,
2. reconciliation,
3. accounting policy,
4. ledger inclusion, and
5. geographic/economic incidence.

Completion of an analytical profile or validation step does not by itself
authorize a federal dollar amount for inclusion in the final fiscal-balance
ledger.

External estimates, including Rockefeller Institute balance-of-payments
results, may be used for diagnostic comparison but are not calibration targets
for WCFFBP results.
